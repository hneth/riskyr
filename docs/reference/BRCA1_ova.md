# Cumulative risk of ovarian cancer in women with the BRCA1 mutation.

`BRCA1_ova` provides the cumulative risk of ovarian cancer in a
population of women with the BRCA1 mutation as a function of their age
(in years).

## Usage

``` r
BRCA1_ova
```

## Format

A data frame (63 x 2).

`age`: age (in years).

`cumRisk`: cumulative risk of developing ovarian cancer in this (BRCA1)
population.

## Source

Based on Figure 2 (p. 2408) of Kuchenbaecker, K. B., Hopper, J. L.,
Barnes, D. R., Phillips, K. A., Mooij, T. M., Roos-Blom, M. J., ... &
BRCA1 and BRCA2 Cohort Consortium (2017). Risks of breast, ovarian, and
contralateral breast cancer for BRCA1 and BRCA2 mutation carriers.
*JAMA*, *317* (23), 2402–2416. doi: 10.1001/jama.2017.7112
